# Why this is a different primitive

The contract does not ask validators to choose a winning outcome. They perform only material comparability across owner-frozen dimensions. Contract code combines that partition with a registered precedent outcome and the proposer's intended outcome to derive consistency.

Unlike document diffing, every dimension is semantic and policy-specific. Unlike dispute resolution, there is no appeal verdict or adjudicator-selected remedy. Unlike an evidence score, any material difference distinguishes the precedent, while complete comparability makes unequal treatment visible and offers exactly one deterministic alignment transition.
